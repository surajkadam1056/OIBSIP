# EDA on Retail Sales Data

## Oasis Infobyte Data Analytics Internship

### Project Overview

This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a retail sales dataset to identify sales patterns, customer behaviour, product performance, and useful business insights.

The analysis was performed using Python libraries such as Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The objective of this project is to explore retail sales data and uncover meaningful patterns and trends that can help businesses make data-driven decisions.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* Python
* Pandas
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn
* Jupyter Notebook

---

## 📂 Dataset

The dataset contains retail sales information, including customer demographics, product details, sales information, and transaction dates.

---

## 📊 Analysis Performed

The following analysis was performed:

### 1. Data Inspection

* Checked dataset shape
* Examined column names and data types
* Checked for missing values

### 2. Descriptive Statistics

Calculated statistical measures for numerical columns:

* Mean
* Median
* Mode
* Standard Deviation

### 3. Time Series Analysis

* Monthly sales trend analysis
* Quarterly sales trend analysis

### 4. Customer Demographics Analysis

* Customer age group distribution
* Gender distribution

### 5. Product Analysis

* Top 10 best-selling products
* Revenue generated by product category

### 6. Correlation Analysis

A correlation heatmap was created to identify relationships between numerical variables.

### 7. Additional Analysis

Additional visualizations were created to identify hidden patterns and provide further business insights.

---

## 📈 Visualizations

The project includes the following visualizations:

* Monthly Sales Trend
* Quarterly Sales Trend
* Customer Age Group Distribution
* Gender Distribution
* Top 10 Best-Selling Products
* Revenue by Product Category
* Correlation Heatmap
* Additional Business Insight Visualization

## 🔍 Key Insights

The Exploratory Data Analysis helped identify:

* Sales trends across different months and quarters.
* Customer purchasing patterns based on age and gender.
* Best-performing products based on sales.
* Product categories generating higher revenue.
* Relationships between important numerical variables.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations can be considered:

1. Focus marketing campaigns on customer groups that show higher purchasing activity.
2. Increase inventory and promotional efforts for top-performing products.
3. Improve strategies for low-performing product categories to increase revenue.
4. Use monthly and quarterly sales trends to improve inventory planning and sales forecasting.
